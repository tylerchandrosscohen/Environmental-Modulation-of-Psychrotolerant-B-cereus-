# **Environmental modulation of cytotoxicity and virulence factor stability in psychrotolerant Bacillus cereus group isolates**

## Tyler Chandross-Cohen, Mackenna Yount, Erin Readinger, Cassidy Prince, Kayla Kimble, Brian Praul, Lydia Weidner, Jasna Kovac

### **Abstract**
The Bacillus cereus group comprises strains relevant to food spoilage, agriculture, and human disease, with psychrotolerant members associated with refrigerated food spoilage. However, the food safety risk posed by these psychrotolerant strains remains poorly understood. This study characterized psychrotolerant B. cereus group strains from panC groups II and VI by quantifying (i) cytotoxicity at different temperatures and media (10, 32, and 37 °C in brain heart infusion broth (BHIB), and 10 °C and 32 °C in skim milk broth (SMB)), (ii) transcription of virulence genes at these same conditions, and (iii) reduction in supernatant cytotoxicity due to protein stressors. Isolates showed higher cytotoxicity (p = 0.00086) when grown in BHIB at 32 °C (0.40 ± 0.23) compared to 37 °C (0.28 ± 0.20). A subset of tested isolates also showed higher cytotoxicity (p = 0.0054) and transcription (p < 0.001) of tested virulence genes at 32 °C than at 10 °C in SMB and BHIB. Thermal (65, 75, 100 °C for 1-2 min), low-pH (2), and trypsin (10 μg/μL) treatments significantly reduced cytotoxicity of supernatants collected from isolates grown at 32 °C in BHIB, though residual cytotoxicity remained above the level of sterile BHIB. This study highlights that psychrotolerant B. cereus isolates express cytotoxic virulence factors, although at a low level, in a dairy food model under conditions mimicking temperature abuse during refrigerated storage. Furthermore, the persistence of residual cytotoxicity following thermal, pH, and proteolytic treatments highlights the need to better understand the biological relevance of these residual activities during human exposure.

**File Explanations**
The file "Figure_1.csv" contains the Newick tree file for all isolates. The file "Figure_2.csv" contains the cytotoxicity data for all isolates at 32 °C and 37 °C. The file "Figure_3.csv" contains the cytotoxicity data for a subset of isolates at 10 °C and 32 °C. The file "Figure_4_raw_data.csv" contains the raw RT-qPCR data, and the file "Figure_4.csv" contains the Log2FC RT-qPCR data for a subset of isolates at 10 °C and 32 °C. The file "Figure_5.csv" contains the cytotoxicity data for a subset of isolates after exposure to protein-destabilizing conditions. The file "Figure_S1.csv" contains the growth data for all isolates at 6 °C. The file "BTyper3_output.csv" contains the BTyper3 report for all isolates. The file "R code for Environmental Modulation of Cytotoxicity and Virulence Factor Stability in Psychrotolerant Bacillus cereus Group Isolates.Rmd" contains an R markdown file with the R code run for the generation of statistics and graphs.
